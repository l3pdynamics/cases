Five things this engagement delivered beyond the headline number

  • 01 Model reconciliation — the credibility catch. Two internal sponsor models, dated the same day, reached opposite conclusions on the build-out exit (+$69K vs. −$399K) — a ~$470K cost basis gap plus a static interest-carry plug versus a formula-driven figure. Resolved before anything went external.
  • 02 Finished Lots de-risked, not just re-run. A shared-cost-allocation error had every exit absorbing full build-out costs. Corrected, the fallback exit moved from a ~$1.1M loss to a ~$111K loss — a materially different risk profile for the safest path.
  • 03 A path back to positive, not just a "no." Three concrete, costed levers were identified to bring the build-out exit to a positive return: horizontal cost reduction, proving the price band with closed comps, and restructuring the capital stack.
  • 04 A 17-item pre-capital-raise checklist. Open exposure the deck was silent on — stormwater and drainage, wetland delineation, impact fee verification, flood zone, title and financing path — each one a potential post-close surprise avoided pre-close.
  • 05 Durable infrastructure, not a one-time number. A rebuilt 13-tab pro forma — zero formula errors, scenario manager, stress-results tab, extended 30-month timeline — is a reusable decision tool for this deal and the template for future engagements.
